The profiles are not available locally and the example.org URLs are not resolvable, so I built the bundle on the standard bindings these profile types almost always use: LOINC for blood pressure, SNOMED CT for the problem, RxNorm for the medication, UCUM for units. If the clinic's profiles bind different value sets, the codes are the only parts that would need swapping.

Decisions reflected in the bundle:

- **Two blood pressure observations** for the visit date, one per reading. The September reading has no day, so it cannot be dated as YYYY-MM-DD and is left out.
- **Family history and other findings** are omitted per the rules. "No regular meds" is not a resource.
- **Hypertension** is coded as SNOMED 38341003. SNOMED has no distinct "stage 2" concept, so the stage goes in the Condition note.
- **Amlodipine 5 mg** uses RxNorm 197361, status active, asserted on the visit date, once daily by mouth.
