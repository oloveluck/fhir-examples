What I assumed and left out:

- **Profiles unreachable.** The example.org StructureDefinitions do not resolve and no copies exist in the working directory. I used the bindings these profile types almost always carry: LOINC for the blood pressure panel and its components, SNOMED CT for problems, RxNorm for medications. If the clinic's profiles bind to different value sets, the codes are the only thing that needs swapping.
- **Date of birth** is read as April 12, 1961. That is the only reading of 04/12/1961 that makes the patient 65 on the visit date, as the note states.
- **Omitted on purpose:** heart rate, weight, the LDL result, morning headaches, and the negated findings for chest pain, shortness of breath, and edema. None of these fit the four profiles.
- **Blood pressure** carries the visit date as its effective date, since the note gives no time.
