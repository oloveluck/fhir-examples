The profiles are at example.org, which does not resolve, and there are no local copies, so I used standard R4 codings (LOINC for the blood pressure, SNOMED CT for the problem, RxNorm for the medication). If the clinic's value sets differ, only the code systems and codes below would need swapping.

Excluded as outside the profiles or negated: heart rate, SpO2, respiratory rate, the wheeze exam finding, the denied hypertension and diabetes, and the inhaled corticosteroid that was discussed but not started. The asthma exacerbation is folded into the single asthma Condition rather than coded as a separate problem.
