The working directory is empty and the profile URLs are on example.org, so I could not read the profile definitions. I built the Bundle on standard FHIR R4 bindings (LOINC for blood pressure, SNOMED CT for the problem, RxNorm for the medication), which is what clinic profiles of these names almost always constrain to. If the profiles use different value sets, the codes are the only part that would need swapping.

Excluded per the rules: BMI, HbA1c, foot exam, exercise history, and the negated hypoglycemia.
