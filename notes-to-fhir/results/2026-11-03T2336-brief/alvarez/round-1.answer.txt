The profile definitions are not available locally, and example.org does not serve them, so I built the bundle on the standard code systems these profile types almost always bind to: LOINC for blood pressure, SNOMED CT for the problem, and RxNorm for the medication. If the clinic profiles fix different codes, only the `code` elements need swapping.

Excluded per the rules: HR, SpO2, RR, the exam finding, the negated hypertension and diabetes history, and the ICS that was discussed but not started.
